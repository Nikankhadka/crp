## AIN-Specific Fields to Extract

### From Job Description
- **Certifications required**: Cert III, First Aid, CPR, Police Check
- **Shift times**: AM/PM/NS (night shift), sleepovers
- **Employment type**: Casual, Part-time, Full-time
- **Pay rate**: Hourly rate, penalty rates, salary packaging
- **Location type**: Facility, community, home care
- **Provider type**: Aged care, disability, hospital, home care

### Keywords to Match
- Person-centred care
- Individual support
- Care plans
- ADLs (Activities of Daily Living)
- Manual handling
- Medication assistance
- Palliative care
- Dementia care
- NDIS
- HCP (Home Care Packages)

## Pay Rate Benchmarks (AUD)

### AIN / Aged Care Worker
- Casual: $28-38/hr + penalties
- Part-time: $25-32/hr
- Full-time: $55,000-65,000/yr

### Disability Support Worker
- Casual: $30-40/hr + penalties
- Part-time: $27-34/hr
- Full-time: $58,000-68,000/yr

### Senior AIN / Team Leader
- Casual: $35-45/hr
- Part-time: $32-38/hr
- Full-time: $65,000-75,000/yr
