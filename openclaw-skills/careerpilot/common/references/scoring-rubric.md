# Scoring Rubric

## Overall Score (0-100)

### Skills Overlap (40 points)
- **35-40**: 90%+ keyword match, all required skills present
- **28-34**: 70-89% keyword match, most required skills present
- **20-27**: 50-69% keyword match, some required skills missing
- **10-19**: 30-49% keyword match, significant gaps
- **0-9**: Less than 30% match, major skill gaps

### Experience Relevance (25 points)
- **22-25**: Direct industry experience, right seniority level, 3+ years relevant
- **17-21**: Related industry experience, right seniority, 2+ years relevant
- **12-16**: Transferable experience, slightly off seniority, 1+ year relevant
- **6-11**: Some transferable skills, wrong seniority level
- **0-5**: No relevant experience

### Location Match (15 points)
- **15**: Perfect match (same city, or remote in same country)
- **12-14**: Commutable distance, or remote with occasional travel
- **8-11**: Requires relocation but feasible
- **4-7**: Major relocation required, international
- **0-3**: Location mismatch, visa issues likely

### Salary Alignment (10 points)
- **10**: Listed salary matches expectations exactly
- **8-9**: Within 10% of expectations
- **6-7**: Within 20% of expectations
- **3-5**: Below expectations but negotiable
- **0-2**: Significantly below expectations, no room for negotiation

### Company Culture Fit (10 points)
- **10**: Perfect alignment with values, size preference, industry
- **8-9**: Strong alignment, minor concerns
- **6-7**: Acceptable fit, some misalignment
- **3-5**: Significant culture concerns
- **0-2**: Major red flags, blacklist proximity

## Score Interpretation

- **85-100**: Excellent match, apply immediately
- **70-84**: Good match, worth applying
- **60-69**: Decent match, apply if high volume day
- **50-59**: Stretch match, only if desperate
- **0-49**: Poor match, skip

## Profile-Specific Thresholds

### Tech Profile
- Minimum: 65
- Target: 75+
- Stretch: 60-64 (apply with tailored cover letter)

### AIN Profile
- Minimum: 60
- Target: 70+
- Stretch: 55-59 (apply if location/salary perfect)
