## AIN-Specific Fields

### Certifications & Requirements
Look for these keywords in job descriptions:
- Certificate III in Individual Support (Ageing/Disability)
- Certificate III in Health Services Assistance
- First Aid Certificate
- CPR Certificate
- Police Check / National Police Certificate
- NDIS Worker Screening
- Working With Children Check
- Driver's Licence
- Reliable vehicle (for community roles)

### Shift Information
AIN roles often specify:
- Morning/Afternoon/Night shifts
- Weekend work required
- Sleepover shifts (community roles)
- Flexible hours
- Casual/Part-time/Full-time

### Pay Rate Indicators
- Hourly rate (e.g., $28-35/hr)
- Penalty rates (weekends, public holidays)
- Salary packaging available (not-for-profit providers)
- Above-award rates

## Rate Limiting

Same as general Seek:
- Wait 2-3 seconds between job card clicks
- Wait 3-5 seconds between full job page loads
- Maximum 20 jobs per session
- If CAPTCHA appears: stop immediately

## Common Differences from General Seek

1. Healthcare jobs may have additional fields (shift times, certifications)
2. Some jobs are listed under "Community Services" instead of "Healthcare"
3. Pay rates are often hourly instead of salary
4. Casual positions are more common in aged care
5. Some providers use "Expression of Interest" instead of direct applications
