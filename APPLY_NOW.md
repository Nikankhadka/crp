## How to Apply

### Option 1: Seek Quick Apply (fastest)
1. Go to seek.com.au
2. Search for the job title and company
3. Click "Quick Apply" or "Apply"
4. Upload the tailored resume (convert .md to .docx/pdf first)
5. Paste the cover letter text
6. Submit

### Option 2: Direct company website
1. Find the job on the company's careers page
2. Fill in the application form
3. Upload materials from the output folder
4. Submit
